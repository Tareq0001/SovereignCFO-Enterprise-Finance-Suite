**SovereignCFO** is an institutional-grade **Enterprise Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), Double-Entry General Ledger (GL), Multi-Entity Consolidation, and ZATCA Phase-2 E-Invoicing** web platform engineered by **Tareq Aboushi**.

---

## Key Capabilities

1. **Executive FP&A Command Center**
   - Symmetrical 4-card executive KPI strip with inline SVG trend sparklines (`Consolidated Revenue`, `EBITDA & Operating Margin`, `Net Profit & ROE`, `Free Cash Flow & Liquidity Ratio`).
   - Interactive **P&L Revenue-to-Net-Income Waterfall Chart** with hover tooltips and real-time What-If macroeconomic stress testing (`Revenue Growth`, `COGS Inflation`, `OPEX Optimization`, `WACC / Discount Rate`).
   - **12-Month Treasury Cash Flow Trajectory** & **Capital Structure / Balance Sheet Allocation Gauge**.

2. **Double-Entry General Ledger & Trial Balance (`Dr = Cr`)**
   - Strict accounting equation verification:
     $$\sum \text{Debits (مدين)} = \sum \text{Credits (دائن)}$$
   - Live voucher entry with automated ledger posting, trial balance recalculation, and instant cascading updates into the Income Statement and Balance Sheet.

3. **IFRS Consolidated Financial Statements**
   - Multi-entity consolidation across **Sovereign Holding Group**, **KSA Cloud & AI Ops**, **Gulf Logistics & Fleet**, and **FinTech Payment Gateway** with automatic intercompany elimination (`-8,400,000 SAR`).
   - Complete **Income Statement (P&L)**, **Balance Sheet**, and **Statement of Cash Flows** with CSV export.

4. **ZATCA Phase-2 (Fatoora) E-Invoicing Engine**
   - Real-time **15% VAT** calculation, **TLV (Tag-Length-Value) Base64** payload encoding (Tags 1–5), **SHA-256 cryptographic stamp simulation**, interactive QR matrix rendering, and printable **Tax Invoice Preview**.
